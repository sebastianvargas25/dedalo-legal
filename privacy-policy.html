Dédalo Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

Dédalo is an app that construction companies and their workers use to manage job sites, hours, materials, tools and payroll. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, who receives it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Dédalo mobile app and web app, including for users in the United States.

How we use information

We use this information only to provide and maintain Dédalo, keep it secure, respond to you, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for targeted advertising. We do not show ads and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

What is required and what is optional

Your name and email are required, because an account cannot be created without them. If you do not accept this policy, you cannot use the app.

Location is optional. If you do not authorize it, you can still clock in, and your company will see that the entry has no location. You may need to choose your job site from a list.

Location

Dédalo reads your location only at two moments: when you clock in or out, to record where you are and identify the job site, and when an administrator sets the location of a job site. Dédalo does not read your location when you open the app, and it does not collect location in the background.

Administrators of your company can see the exact point where you clocked in or out on a map, how far it was from the job site, and whether it was inside the site's radius. When someone opens that point on a map, the coordinates are sent to Google Maps.

You can withdraw your authorization at any time in Settings, and you can also turn location off in your phone's settings. Withdrawing applies from then on and does not erase earlier entries.

Accuracy of hours and location

Clock-in times and locations come from the phone. A phone's clock or GPS can be wrong, delayed or altered, and Dédalo does not independently verify them. Entries made without signal are sent later. Companies should review these records before relying on them for payroll or any other decision.

Who can see your information

You can see your own hours. Administrators of the company you work for can see your information within that company. If you work with more than one company, one company cannot see your information in another.

Service providers

We use the following providers so the service can work. They process information on our behalf:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage.
  • Expo: delivery of push notifications and app updates.
  • Brevo: account emails, such as password recovery.
  • Google Drive: storage of backups, which are encrypted before they leave our systems.
  • Google Maps: receives coordinates only when someone opens a point on a map.
  • Have I Been Pwned: checks whether a new password has appeared in known data breaches. It receives only a fragment of a hash of the password, never the password itself.
  • Apple: distributes the app and delivers notifications to iPhones.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights, property or safety of users or others.

Where information is stored

Dédalo is operated from Costa Rica. Our main database is hosted in Canada, and our providers may process information in the United States and other countries. By using Dédalo, you understand that your information may be stored and processed outside your state or country.

Information stored on your phone

Dédalo works without signal. To do that, it keeps on your phone a limited copy of the information you work with, entries you made without signal, and copies of attached photos until they finish uploading. This local database has no encryption of its own: it is protected by your phone's lock. Your session is encrypted with a key kept in your phone's secure storage. On the web version, your session is erased when you close the tab.

Hours, expenses and requests that were already sent are removed from the phone the next time you open the app. Cached data and sent job logs are removed when you sign out. Anything not yet sent stays on the phone, even if you sign out, so that hours worked are not lost, and it is sent when you sign back in with that account. Everything is removed when you uninstall the app.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep your information while your account is active.

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete my account, without contacting anyone. Deletion is immediate and permanent, and it applies to every company you belong to. It removes your access, your profile, your hours and locations, your pay records and receipts, your advances, your expenses and requests, and your consent record. It cannot be undone. Job sites and general company information are not deleted.

Encrypted backups are deleted after 30 days, so deleted information may remain in a backup until then.

If you cannot sign in, email sebvarcu25@gmail.com to request deletion.

Information for companies

If your company uses Dédalo to manage workers, your company decides how it uses workers' hours, pay and location records, and it is responsible for meeting its own obligations, including giving workers any required notice about location tracking and keeping any employment or payroll records the law requires. A worker can delete their account at any time, and that deletes their records. Companies must keep their own copy of any record they are legally required to keep. Dédalo is not a legal record-keeping system and does not provide legal, tax or employment advice.

Security

Access to company data requires an account, and access rules in the database limit what each person can see. Connections are encrypted, and backups are encrypted. We also check new passwords against known data breaches. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Despite these measures, errors, failures, unauthorized access and hacking can happen, and information could be lost, altered or exposed. Please use a strong password and keep your phone locked.

If a security breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and any required authorities as required by the applicable law.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask whether we hold your personal information, to access it, to correct it, to delete it, to receive a copy, and to withdraw your consent. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of.

To make a request, email sebvarcu25@gmail.com. Requests are free. We may need to confirm your identity first. We aim to respond within 5 business days, and we will always respond within the time the applicable law requires. If we deny a request, you can reply to appeal, and we will review it. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

If you work for a company that uses Dédalo, we may refer some requests to that company.

California

California residents can review and change their information as described above. We describe any material change to this policy in the section on changes below. Dédalo does not share personal information with third parties for their own direct marketing.

South Carolina

If a security breach involves the personal identifying information of South Carolina residents, we will notify affected residents as required by S.C. Code § 39-1-90, in the most expedient time possible and without unreasonable delay.

Dédalo is not designed to collect Social Security numbers, driver's license numbers, or bank or card account numbers. Please do not enter them in notes, descriptions or other free-text fields, or include them in photos.

If you have a concern, contact us first at sebvarcu25@gmail.com. You may also contact the South Carolina Department of Consumer Affairs.

Children

Dédalo is a work tool for adults and is not directed to children. You must be at least 18 to use it. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, email sebvarcu25@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Do Not Track

Dédalo does not track users across third-party websites or apps over time, so it does not change its behavior in response to Do Not Track signals.
